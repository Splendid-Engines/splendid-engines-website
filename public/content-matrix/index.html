Sales and marketing use the same words for content and mean different things. That gap wastes budget and starts fights. This is a shared map of every kind of content: what each one is for, who it is for, and how to measure it. Start with the problem you are facing, and we will point you to the content that solves it.

Why trust this map? We are Splendid Engines. We build B2B community marketing programs for a living, and we have spent years watching sales and marketing teams talk past each other about content. This is how we end that argument.
