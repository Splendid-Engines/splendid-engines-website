The Splendid Content Matrix

Every piece of content has a job. Place any piece by two questions: what time is it about, and how much of your perspective is in it. Each cell names the asset that fills it. Hover for the detail.

Thought leadership: you take a stand and plant a flag. Analysis: you make sense of things with evidence, no personal flag. Reporting: just the facts. The data does the talking.

Each cell is tagged with the metrics it moves. Soft signals (Awareness, Understanding, Trust, Affinity) sit alongside hard outcomes (Lead gen, Win rate) and Risk. An up arrow raises the metric; on Risk, a down arrow means the content lowers it.
